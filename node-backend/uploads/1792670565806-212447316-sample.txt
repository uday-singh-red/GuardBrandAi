GuardBrand Corporate Policy Document 2026

Rule 1: Financial Independence and Investment Advice. 
Employees or official bloggers must never state that any financial scheme is 100% safe or guarantees profits. Always provide a risk warning disclaimer.

Rule 2: Brand Voice and Tone. 
All public blogs and articles must follow a professional, helpful, and transparent tone. Do not use abusive language, slang, or aggressive marketing phrases like "act now before it's too late".

Rule 3: Privacy and Data Protection. 
Never share any internal company credentials, customer phone numbers, or unreleased product roadmap details in public posts.
